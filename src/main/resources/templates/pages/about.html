About DeepFij

DeepFij is college basketball, quantified: scores, schedules, box scores, power ratings, and machine-learning game predictions for every Division I program, updated daily through the season.

Predictions come from a family of models — classical Massey and Bradley-Terry ratings alongside gradient-boosted ML models — and every pre-game prediction is scored against the final result and the closing line, in public, on the model performance page.

Game data is sourced from ESPN's public APIs. DeepFij is an independent analytics project and is not affiliated with ESPN or the NCAA. This is not betting advice.
